About Us

Kolkata's Trusted Riding Academy

Since 2018, Seekho Two Wheeler Academy has helped thousands learn scooty and bike riding with patience, safety and real-road confidence.

Our Mission

To empower every learner — especially women and non-cyclists — with the skills and confidence to ride independently and safely on Kolkata roads.

Our Journey

2018
Founded with a vision to make two-wheeler training accessible to everyone.
2020
Expanded ladies-only batches and non-cyclist programs across Kolkata.
2023
Opened multiple branches with EV training and RTO practice modules.
Today
5000+ confident riders trained with 4.9★ Google reviews.
